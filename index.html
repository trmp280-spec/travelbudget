Privacy Policy for TravelBudget

Last updated: October 2, 2026

TravelBudget ("the App") is a travel budget planning app. This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

Data Storage

All data you enter in the App — trip details, budgets, members, and expenses — is stored locally on your device only, using a local database. This data is never uploaded to, or stored on, any server operated by us.

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or have access to any of your personal information, trip data, or expense records. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services of any kind.

Internet Access

The App uses internet access only for the following purposes:

  • Sharing: When you choose to share a trip summary, the App hands the text to your device's built-in share feature (e.g. WhatsApp, Messages, Email). We do not see or store what you share.
  • Update checks: When you tap "Check for updates," the App checks a public version file to see if a newer version is available. No personal data is sent during this check.

Permissions

The App may request internet access permission for the purposes described above. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored only on your device, uninstalling the App permanently deletes all data associated with it. There is no account or server-side data for us to delete.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children, as it does not collect information from anyone.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL.
